Before you plug anything in

Four things that cost boards
Pin 1 is the square pad

Every header on the board marks pin 1 with a square pad; the tables here number from it. The 2×5 motor headers and the 3‑pin servo headers are unshrouded — they will go on backwards if you let them.

Servo rail is 6.0 V

J4/J5 centre pin is the PS2 rail at 6.0 V — the one voltage both jumbo servos accept: Hitec HS‑805BB (4.8–6.0 V) and JX PDI‑HV2060MG (6.0–8.4 V). Either plugs in.

Motor current never touches this board

The two DFR0601 drivers take battery power on their own P+/P− terminals straight from the pack. The board only feeds their 3.3 V logic over the ribbon. J1 feeds the bucks and the amp, behind a 10 A fuse.

Power off to connect

Seat and unseat every connector with the XT60 unplugged. The RP2350 and ESP32 GPIOs are 3.3 V and not forgiving of a 5 V or 6 V pin landing on them during a mis‑plug.

From v9.15

Which of your existing cables still fit
Dome encoder → J8Same

B · A · GND · 5V, identical to DOME_ENC. Plug the old cable in.

Hall → J9Same

3V3 · GND · SIG as before.

Body NeoPixel → J10Same

5V · GND · DATA as before.

Motor ribbons → J2 / J3Regrouped

Same 2×5 format and row order. But J2 = Drive + S2S, J3 = Flywheel + Dome — connect each driver to the right header.

IMU → J6Same plug, new device

Same 4‑pin order (VCC GND · · ). The far end now goes to a BMI160 over I²C instead of the Trinket over UART — re‑terminate that end.

Servos → J4 / J5Split

Was one 2×3 block. Now two 1×3 headers, still 6 V — your HS‑805BBs plug straight on.

S2S pot → J75‑pin now

Pot still works on pins 1–3 (3V3 GND OUT). Pins 4–5 add I²C for the AS5600.

5V_IN / 6V_IN screw terminalsGone

The bucks are on the board now. One XT60 in.

AUDIO_OUT / AUDIO_AUXReplaced

DFPlayer feeds the MAX9744 on‑board; speakers leave on J13/J14.

Slip ring J11 · E‑stop J15 · I²C J17New

No v9.15 equivalent.
